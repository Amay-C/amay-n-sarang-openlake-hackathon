Our Domains

Aero-modelling

We build UAVs and drones completely from scratch. Going beyond standard RC flight, we implement Computer Vision (CV) and Artificial Intelligence to teach our drones spatial awareness, creating advanced obstacle-avoiding autonomous systems for real-world applications.

Astronomy & Astrophysics

We routinely organize sky-watching sessions and professional astrophotography workshops. We are heavily focused on integrating AI and ML (utilizing Astropy) for advanced celestial tracking, object finding, and stellar data analysis.

Reaching the Edge of Space

The High-Altitude Weather Balloon Project

Our most ambitious ongoing project involves designing and launching high-altitude weather balloons to touch the edge of space.

Equipped with custom telemetry, AI-stabilized camera payloads, and GPS tracking, this initiative aims to capture stunning, high-resolution imagery of the Earth's curvature while retrieving vital atmospheric data.
